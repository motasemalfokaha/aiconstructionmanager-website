Account Management

Delete Your Account

AI Construction Manager provides an in-app account deletion option and an external request method.

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Option 1 — Delete your account in the App

If you can access AI Construction Manager, use the in-app account deletion option. Open the App, go to Settings, open the account section, and choose Delete Account.

For security, you may be required to re-authenticate before deletion is completed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the App, send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your AI Construction Manager account. Requests are handled manually; sending an email does not automatically delete an account or any associated records.

Email: support@aiconstructionmanager.app
Request Account Deletion

What account deletion covers

The App provides account deletion and may require re-authentication. The current process deletes the Firebase Authentication account, then removes the selected account's local project records, numbering and project-screen settings on that device, and attempts to remove App-managed project images. Local cleanup may fail after the authentication account has been deleted.

This process does not currently remove locally stored Engi conversations, setup information, drafts or other Engi records, or server-side usage, cost, security, purchase, entitlement or audit records. It does not erase exported reports, copies held by other applications or recipients, data on other devices, or provider-side records and backups.

Data stored only on a device we do not control cannot be remotely removed through an email request. You can use applicable App, device or operating-system controls to remove local data from devices you possess. Take care to preserve any information you still need.

Provider-side information, logs and backups may remain under the applicable provider arrangements and policies. This page does not promise deletion of every copy or a fixed retention period.

If a Google Play subscription is available and you have subscribed, deleting your App account does not itself cancel that subscription.

Identity verification

We may need to verify that you control the account before processing a deletion request. Do not send your password or purchase tokens. We will never ask you to provide your account password by email.
