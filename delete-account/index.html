Account Management

Delete Your Account

AI Construction Manager provides an in-app account deletion option and an external request method.

Option 1 — Delete your account in the App

If you can access AI Construction Manager, use the in-app account deletion option. Open the App, go to Settings, open the account section, and choose Delete Account.

For security, you may be required to re-authenticate before deletion is completed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the App, send an account-deletion request from the email address associated with your AI Construction Manager account.

Email: support@aiconstructionmanager.app
Request Account Deletion

What is deleted

After a successful deletion, the App is designed to delete the Firebase Authentication account and App-managed local project data associated with that user on the device where the deletion is performed.

Because project-management data in the current version is primarily stored locally on the device, data that exists only on a device we do not control cannot be remotely removed from that device through an email request. Users should clear or remove local App data from devices they still possess when appropriate.

Information processed by third-party providers may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or systems according to those providers' retention practices and applicable law.

Identity verification

We may need to verify that you control the account before processing a deletion request. Do not send your password. We will never ask you to provide your account password by email.
